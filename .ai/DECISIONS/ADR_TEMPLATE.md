## Context
Describe the context and problem statement in a few sentences, e.g., the issue we're facing that requires an architectural decision. Explain the forces at play, including technological, business, and team factors. What is the current situation? Why does it need to change?

## Decision
Clearly state the architectural decision being made. Be precise and definitive. Use active voice (e.g., "We will use...", "We are adopting...").

## Rationale
Explain *why* this decision was made. What are the key drivers? 
- Technical advantages (e.g., performance, scalability, maintainability)
- Business alignment (e.g., time-to-market, cost, strategic fit)
- Team considerations (e.g., existing skills, learning curve, tooling support)
If relevant, explain why this choice is better than the alternatives for our specific context.
